Questions and details to bring along

A simple checklist can keep a coverage conversation focused. Before you call, write down what you need to know and collect any materials that might help explain your current situation.

You may want to have:

  • Your current Medicare or other insurance information
  • Any notices about a change in coverage or timing
  • A current prescription list
  • Questions about doctors, providers, or services you use
  • The points you want to compare before making a choice

Ask which details are confirmed and which ones you should verify yourself. If you hear a term you don’t understand, ask for a plain explanation. Avoid choosing based on a single feature without looking at the other parts that matter to you. Keep notes so you can review the conversation later and follow up on unanswered items.
