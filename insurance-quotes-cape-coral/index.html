What to prepare for your quote review

The information needed can vary with the type of policy. To make your conversation more focused, gather what you have and note what still needs checking. A simple checklist:

  • The type of insurance you want to discuss, such as home, auto, health, or life
  • Basic details about the home, vehicle, or person the coverage would relate to
  • Any current policy information you want to compare
  • Questions about coverage, limits, or terms that you want explained
  • Your preferred language for the conversation

Avoid guessing when a detail is uncertain. Tell the agent what you know and ask what else is needed. Another common mistake is comparing only the amount due without checking what coverage is described. Keep the quote information together so you can review the options side by side and ask about differences.
